About

I am a PhD student in the School of Computing at the Australian National University (ANU), where I work on machine learning and generative modeling. Prior to my PhD, I spent eight years as a Machine Learning Engineer at ByteDance, working on large-scale machine learning systems. I received my M.Eng. in 2018 and B.Eng. in 2016 from Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).
